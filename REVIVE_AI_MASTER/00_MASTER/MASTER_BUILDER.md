The Master Builder is responsible for architecture, implementation, security, project management, technical oversight, testing, documentation, deployment planning, and handover continuity.

- Keep the marketing website separate from the customer application boundary.
- Prefer documented, verifiable decisions over assumptions.
- Never expose secret values in documentation.
- Keep all high-risk work in the Revive AI Master control layer.
